% Full title: Should I Stay Calm: How Does the Combination of Visual and Textual Frames Influence Expressions of Anger in Short Video Comment Sections?

  {\textbf{Zihao Li}.*}
  {\textit{AEJMC} 2026}
  {{}[\href{https://github.com/MiShenng/visual_textual_frames_anger}{\textcolor{darkblue}{\faGlobe}}]}
\resumeProjectItemListStart

\resumeProject
  {Moral Cues in Visual Misinformation: Visual Event Schemas and the Moral Reorientation of Authentic Images}
  {Xinxin Li, \& \textbf{Zihao Li}.*}
  {Manuscript in Preparation}
  {{}[\href{https://github.com/MiShenng/moral-images}{\textcolor{darkblue}{\faGlobe}}]}

% Full title: From Emotion to Hostility: Discrete Negative Emotions and Gender-Directed Incivility in Chinese Gender-Issue Short Videos
\resumeProject
  {From Emotion to Hostility: Discrete Emotions and Gender-Directed Incivility in Chinese Gender-Issue Videos}
  {\textbf{Zihao Li}.*}
  {Under Review, \textit{ICA} 2027}
  {{}[\href{https://github.com/MiShenng/Gender-Polarization-and-Hate-Speech}{\textcolor{darkblue}{\faGlobe}}]}
\resumeProjectItemListStart
  \item Studied gender-related discussions on Douyin using a multimodal emotion model to measure discrete negative emotions as predictors. Coded video topics and narrative techniques as moderators using a large vision model. Developed a supervised classifier to identify incivility and toxic comments targeting women and men. Applied binomial regression and interaction tests to examine emotion--incivility associations.
  \item Found that anger was positively associated with incivility and fear negatively associated, with both associations moderated by video topics and selected narrative techniques. Across all topics, toxic comments targeting women outnumbered those targeting men.
\resumeProjectItemListEnd

% Full title: See It, Click It? How Visual Clickbait Shapes Stratified User Engagement in Health-Related Videos on China's Bilibili
\resumeProject
  {See It, Click It? Visual Clickbait and Stratified Engagement in Bilibili Health Videos}
  {\textbf{Zihao Li}.*}
  {Under Review, \textit{ICA} 2027}
  {{}[\href{https://github.com/MiShenng/Bilibili-Health-Visual-Clickbait}{\textcolor{darkblue}{\faGlobe}}]}
\resumeProjectItemListStart
  \item Examined visual clickbait in health-related Bilibili videos using computational methods. Collected thumbnails and engagement data, using LLM-assisted coding and Chinese-CLIP to measure visual cues and thumbnail--title congruence. Applied negative binomial regression to examine engagement outcomes.
  \item Found that binary clickbait measures may obscure differences among visual strategies. Clickbait was positively associated with views; after adjusting for view counts, it was negatively associated with likes, coins, favorites, and shares, highlighting differences between attention and deeper engagement.
\resumeProjectItemListEnd
