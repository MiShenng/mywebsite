Profile

My name is Li Zao, and I am a fourth-year undergraduate majoring in Journalism and Communication at Lanzhou University. I am working toward becoming a social science researcher who uses quantitative and computational methods to describe social phenomena, draw inferences, and better understand the world. Through this online space, I hope to connect with others who share similar interests.

My current research interests center on three areas: (i) how visual content evokes emotions and shapes people’s judgments about and engagement with public issues, (ii) how political actors use social bots to produce and circulate visual propaganda, including images and memes, and (iii) causal inference in natural experimental settings, as well as the use of AI in data generation processes.

I am currently struggling my way through applications to research-oriented master’s and Ph.D. programs in the hope of keeping my research career alive.
