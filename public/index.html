Profile

Welcome to my website — my backstage. My name is Li Zao and I am a third-year undergraduate student majoring in Journalism at Lanzhou University. I am working toward becoming a social science researcher who uses quantitative and computational methods to describe, infer, and better understand the world.

My current research and academic interests focus on visual culture and communication, political communication, social media and society, as well as computational communication methodologies.

Through this online space, I hope to connect with others who share similar interests. I'm currently applying to graduate school to pursue a master's degree. Wish me luck!
