description: |
  Existing data shows that sales of scratch-off lottery tickets are growing rapidly in China, with young people becoming the main consumers. Why are they so interested in scratch-offs? And what does this trend tell us about the mindset of young people in China?

  This project combines lottery sales data, industry reports, and social media discussions to explore why scratch-offs have become so popular. From the perspective of youth culture, it looks at how pressure, instant gratification, and the appeal of low-cost hope shape this consumer trend.

  This project was completed as the final assignment for a computer graphics and design course.
