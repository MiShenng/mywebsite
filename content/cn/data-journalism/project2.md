  AI companions are becoming part of more people’s intimate lives. But why are they so appealing? Is love between humans and AI a new kind of relationship, or simply a substitute for relationships between people?

  This project looks at user needs and product design, together with discussions on social media, to explore why people form emotional relationships with AI and how algorithms create a sense of intimacy. It also discusses the dependence and boundaries that may come with these digital relationships.

  This project won Second Prize in the 2025 China Data Journalism Competition.
