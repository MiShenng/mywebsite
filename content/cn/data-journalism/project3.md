description: |
  China’s concert market is booming, but “I couldn’t get a ticket” has also become a common experience. Why are concert tickets so hard to get? And why does it sometimes feel like every popular concert sells out instantly?

  Using data on China’s live performance market, ticket prices, and ticket sales, this project explores the supply and demand behind the concert boom. It looks at which artists are easier to get tickets for, how ticket availability differs across regions, and how ticketing systems, platforms, and the resale market affect ordinary concertgoers.

  This was a campus media project, made just for fun.
